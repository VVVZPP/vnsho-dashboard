Power isn't a performance stat here. It's a tax band.

Every brochure leads with horsepower. In Singapore that same figure, in kilowatts, is what LTA charges your road tax on — and what decides which COE queue you bid in. Move the slider to any car's power figure and see the bill before you commit to it.

What it costs to keep on the road.

Road tax plus electricity. Both are knowable to the dollar before you buy — unlike the purchase price, which moves with every COE round.

Range is the wrong headline.

The median Singapore car covers about 48 km a day. Measured in days of ordinary driving rather than kilometres, the gap between the shortest- and longest-range EV on sale stops looking like a decision.

The charger you live with is the slow one.

Nine in ten charges happen overnight on AC. The DC peak figure brands compete on only matters on the days you can't plug in at home — and it's throttled by the onboard charger the rest of the time.
